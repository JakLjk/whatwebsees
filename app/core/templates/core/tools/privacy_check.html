What these signals mean

Do Not Track

DNT expresses a preference, but it is not technical enforcement. Websites and other recipients decide how they respond, and some browsers no longer expose a meaningful value.

Global Privacy Control

GPC is a privacy preference signal intended to communicate that a person does not want their data sold or shared. Its legal effect depends on the applicable jurisdiction and recipient.

Cookie availability

navigator.cookieEnabled is a broad browser indication. It does not prove that every cookie or storage mechanism works in every context, and this tool does not set a test cookie.

Why results vary

Browsers, extensions, managed policies and privacy modes can alter or hide these values. An unavailable result is not an error and does not necessarily mean a preference is disabled.

For adjacent signals, inspect the browser's reported User-Agent, then run the local canvas and WebGL checks. Those values can contribute to fingerprinting, but none should be treated as a stable identity on its own.
