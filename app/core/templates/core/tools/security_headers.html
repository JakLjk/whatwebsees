What the main security headers do

HTTPS and content controls

Strict-Transport-Security (HSTS) asks browsers to use HTTPS for future visits. Content-Security-Policy (CSP) limits where scripts, styles, images and other resources may come from. X-Content-Type-Options tells browsers not to reinterpret declared file types.

Privacy and browser features

Referrer-Policy controls how much referring-address information is sent during navigation. Permissions-Policy controls access to selected browser features such as camera, microphone and location.

Cross-origin isolation

COOP, COEP and CORP control how documents and resources interact across origins. They are powerful but are not appropriate for every site, so their absence is not automatically a vulnerability.
