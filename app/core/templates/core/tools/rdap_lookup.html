What RDAP can tell you

RDAP is the structured successor to legacy WHOIS. It can provide creation, change and expiration events, registry status codes, nameservers and registrar information. Some fields may be absent or redacted.

Why dates and status values need context

Registry practices differ by top-level domain, and renewal or grace periods may not be obvious from one expiration date. A domain can also change owners or content without changing its original registration date.

Compare registration context with the domain's current DNS records, its public addresses in Hostname Lookup, or the focused Domain Age Checker.

Technical details

The checker discovers the appropriate HTTPS RDAP service through IANA, accepts only public destinations and intentionally does not display personal registrant fields.
