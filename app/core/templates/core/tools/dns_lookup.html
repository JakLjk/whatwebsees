How to read DNS records

The Domain Name System publishes structured records through distributed authoritative servers and recursive resolvers. A and AAAA map names to IPv4 and IPv6; CNAME aliases one name to another; MX selects mail servers; NS identifies authoritative name servers; TXT carries text-based policies or verification data; and SOA describes the zone's authority and timing values.

Why results can differ

Resolver caches retain answers for their TTL, so recently changed records can appear different until older answers expire. Geographic traffic routing, resolver policy and DNSSEC validation can also affect what a resolver returns.
