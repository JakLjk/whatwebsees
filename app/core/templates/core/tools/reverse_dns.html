What reverse DNS means

A PTR record maps an IP address back to a hostname. The owner of the address range controls the reverse zone, so the result may differ from names that point to the same address.

Why an address may have no PTR record

Reverse records are optional and many addresses have none. Absence does not mean an address is broken, though correctly configured PTR records can matter for mail delivery and operational diagnostics.

PTR is not identity proof

A returned hostname is published metadata. It does not authenticate the service currently running at that address.
