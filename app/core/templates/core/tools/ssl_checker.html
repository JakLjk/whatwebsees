The server connects only to already resolved and validated public IP addresses. Every DNS candidate must be public; no arbitrary ports or protocols are accepted.

What a TLS certificate verifies

A browser-trusted certificate connects one or more hostnames to a public key through a trusted certificate authority. Successful validation means the chain is trusted, the certificate is currently within its validity window and its names cover the hostname checked.

Issuer, hostname and expiration

The issuer is the certificate authority that signed the certificate. Subject Alternative Name entries list the hostnames it covers, and the validity dates show when clients should begin and stop accepting it. A certificate can fail because it expired, its chain is incomplete or the requested hostname is missing.

A valid certificate is not a trust rating

HTTPS protects the connection and confirms control of the hostname during certificate issuance. It does not prove that a site's content, downloads, business or operator is honest or safe.
