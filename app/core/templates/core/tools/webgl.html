The WebGL check runs entirely in your browser. GPU and capability values are not sent to Django or stored.

What WebGL tells a website

WebGL is a browser graphics API used for interactive 2D and 3D content. A webpage can ask the graphics context about supported versions, limits and a renderer description.

Why graphics information is privacy-relevant

Renderer names and capability limits can contribute one signal to browser fingerprinting. They are not normally unique on their own, and virtual machines, drivers and browser translation layers can change what appears.

Why information may be unavailable

Browsers can hide, standardize or substitute graphics details to protect privacy or improve compatibility. Disabled hardware acceleration, blocklists and hardened settings can also make WebGL unavailable.

Compare this output with the Canvas Fingerprint Test, the current screen and viewport measurements, and the broader Browser Checker. The Privacy Check covers explicit preference signals rather than graphics capabilities.
